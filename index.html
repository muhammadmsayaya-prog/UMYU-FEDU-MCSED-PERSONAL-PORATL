ABOUT

ABOUT THE DEPARTMENT

Education and Computer Science Education information.

EDUCATION STUDENTS

Education students are future teachers, mentors, and leaders. Your work is about more than delivering lessons—it is about understanding learners, building inclusive classrooms, assessing growth, and helping students believe in themselves. Stay reflective, ethical, and adaptable. The impact you make may not always be immediate, but it lasts.

COMPUTER SCIENCE EDUCATION STUDENTS

You stand at the intersection of education and technology. You must learn how people learn while also mastering programming, algorithms, data, and digital systems. Your role is to make computing accessible, not intimidating—teaching problem-solving, computational thinking, and responsible technology use.

ABOUT

MATHEMATICS EDUCATION STUDENTS

You are training to make mathematics clear, logical and enjoyable for the next generation. Alongside core mathematics you study how learners build number sense and abstract reasoning — strong content knowledge plus strong pedagogy is what makes a great mathematics teacher.

RULES & REGULATIONS

General academic conduct expectations.

Attendance: A minimum of 75% lecture attendance is required to sit for examinations.
Academic Integrity: Examination malpractice and plagiarism attract disciplinary action.
Dress Code: Students are expected to dress decently within the department and campus.
Assignments: Late submissions may attract a penalty unless prior approval is obtained.
Examinations: Students must carry a valid ID card to every examination.
